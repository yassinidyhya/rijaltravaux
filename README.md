# Rijal Travaux

A professional corporate website for **Société Rijal Travaux et Services**, a Moroccan construction company specializing in public infrastructure, educational facilities, civil engineering, and construction services.

[Visit the live website](https://rijaltravaux.idyhya.dev?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

## Overview

Rijal Travaux is a French-language corporate website designed to present the company's services, expertise, projects, and contact information to clients and partners in Morocco.

The project focuses on a clean, responsive presentation of the company's construction activities and completed work.

## Features

* Responsive corporate design
* French-language interface
* Company presentation
* Construction and civil engineering services
* Project portfolio
* Project detail pages
* Contact and quotation request form
* Company contact information
* Legal and privacy pages
* Mobile-friendly navigation
* SEO-friendly page structure
* Custom 404 page

## Services

The website presents services including:

* Construction
* Public infrastructure
* Educational facilities
* Civil engineering
* Well drilling
* Earthworks
* Renovation
* Finishing and development
* Administrative and government buildings

## Projects

The project includes dedicated pages for selected construction projects, including:

* Lycée Collégial Baby à Boujdour
* École Communautaire ANFEG

Project pages provide relevant information such as location, year, client, and project scope.
